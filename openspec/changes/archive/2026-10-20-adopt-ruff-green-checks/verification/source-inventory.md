generated from repository-owned source under `docker/`, `tests/` and `scripts/`.

## Scope and selection rules

- Roots: `docker/`, `tests/`, `scripts/`.
- Included: every `.py` and `.pyi` file under those roots, plus extensionless
  scripts whose first line is a Python shebang.
- Traversal is sorted; directory symlinks are not followed (none exist under the
  scoped roots at this revision).
- `.venv`, `.git`, `.pytest_cache`, `.docker-cache`, `.docker-generated` and
  `.docker-local` are outside the scoped roots and are not traversed.
- `openspec/**` is historical planning/verification evidence and is excluded by
  the change contract.
